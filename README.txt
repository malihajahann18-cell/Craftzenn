CRAFT ZEN — PINTEREST-INSPIRED GIFT WEBSITE
===========================================

FILES
- index.html       Main website structure (HTML + Tailwind CDN)
- style.css        Custom pastel pink/cream design and responsive layouts
- script.js        Search, categories, wishlist, shopping bag and WhatsApp order flow
- assets/          Your supplied Craft Zen logo and product images

PRODUCT PHOTO MAPPING
1. Chocolate Bouquet — first quadrant of your 4-photo collage
2. Ferrero Rocher Bouquet — second quadrant of your 4-photo collage
3. Single Polaroids — third quadrant of your 4-photo collage
4. Polaroids Bouquet — fourth quadrant of your 4-photo collage
5. Gift Basket — your separate basket photo
6. Gift Box — your separate gift box photo

HOW TO RUN
1. Extract the ZIP.
2. Open the Craft_Zen_Final folder in VS Code.
3. Open index.html with Live Server (recommended). Internet is needed for Tailwind CDN and Google Fonts.

IMPORTANT: SET YOUR WHATSAPP NUMBER
Open script.js and replace:
  const WHATSAPP_NUMBER = '01884047422';
with your own number including Bangladesh country code 880, without +, spaces, or dashes.
Example format only: 01884047422
